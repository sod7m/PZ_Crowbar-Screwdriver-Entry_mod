title=Crowbar & Screwdriver Entry
description=[h1]Crowbar & Screwdriver Entry[/h1]
description=A locked door shouldn't be the end of the road. Three vanilla tools, three ways through it — loud, quiet, or somewhere in between. No new items, no map edits, nothing that opens by itself.
description=
description=[hr][/hr]
description=[h2]Crowbar — loud, but it always has an answer[/h2]
description=Force open locked doors, windows and garage doors, and locked vehicle doors and hatches. Success scales with [b]Strength[/b], [b]Fitness[/b] and the crowbar's condition — a fresh survivor with a good crowbar gets in roughly half the time, and it only climbs from there.
description=Every failed swing chews through the crowbar's condition, makes noise the neighbours will hear, and carries a chance of a nasty knock to the hand. Double doors and garage doors unlock as a whole, not one panel at a time.
description=
description=[h2]Screwdriver — quiet, patient, skill-hungry[/h2]
description=Work the lock instead of the frame. Almost silent, no injury risk, barely any wear — but the odds start low and lean hard on [b]Nimble[/b], with [b]Mechanics[/b] and [b]Fitness[/b] chipping in. Burglars get a standing bonus. Works on doors and on vehicle doors and hatches.
description=Bring spares. A screwdriver is cheap; the twenty minutes you spend crouched in the open are not.
description=Which locks it may touch is up to the server: house doors, garage doors, vehicle doors and security doors are four separate switches.
description=
description=[h2]Bolt cutters — the tool does the work[/h2]
description=Cut the lock clean off any locked door or gate. Bolt cutters are the most reliable of the three (the leverage is mechanical, not muscular) and by far the loudest — and unlike the crowbar, they'll go through reinforced security doors.
description=They also cut [b]wire fences[/b]. Pick a chain-link or barbed-wire panel, cut through it, and the panel is gone — a permanent hole in the perimeter, with the salvaged wire dropped at your feet. Pipe-only and plank panels are off the menu, because bolt cutters cut wire, not steel tube.
description=
description=[hr][/hr]
description=[h2]Reinforced doors[/h2]
description=Vault and security doors are their own class, with a separate toggle per tool. Out of the box neither the crowbar nor the screwdriver can touch them; switch either on and it also has to clear a skill level — Strength for the crowbar, Nimble for the screwdriver. Bolt cutters can take them by default (toggle that off, or put a Strength requirement on them too). Force one open and the game hands you a working key, so you never have to break in twice.
description=
description=[h2]Tuned however you like — 28 sandbox options[/h2]
description=[list]
description=[*]Master switch, plus a separate toggle per tool
description=[*]Success multipliers for prying, lockpicking and cutting
description=[*]Noise multipliers — independently, per tool
description=[*]Action time multiplier, and an extra one for fences
description=[*]Tool wear on failure (set it to 0 and tools never wear out)
description=[*]Injury chance and injury severity, separately
description=[*]Vehicle doors, garage doors and reinforced doors switchable per tool — the crowbar, the screwdriver and the bolt cutters each get their own set
description=[*]Skill gates on reinforced doors: Strength for the crowbar, Nimble for the screwdriver, optional Strength for the cutters
description=[*]Fence cutting and wire salvage, switchable on their own
description=[/list]
description=Every value listed above is a real knob, not decoration. Turn the mod into a brutal grind or a convenience feature; both are one settings screen away.
description=

description=Server-authoritative by design. Every roll, unlock, fence removal, tool wear and injury is resolved and validated on the server, with distance checks and request de-duplication — the client asks, it never decides.
description=
